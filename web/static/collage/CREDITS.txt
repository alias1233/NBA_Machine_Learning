Collage credits

Every image in `static/collage/` should have a row here.

A row records the file name, where the image came from, its licence, and who made it. If the image
was generated rather than sourced, the source column says so and names the tool.

Before adding a row, look at the image at full size and check it against R8.2: no player faces, no
legible jersey number or name, no team or league mark, no arena signage, and no readable brand.
